# Rewynd

Record, clip, edit and share your games — light on your PC.

- **Instant replay** — save the last moments after something cool happens (Alt+C)
- **Auto clips** — Rewynd saves big moments by itself while you play
- **Record** full screen or just one app, with each sound on its own track
- **Editor + Studio** — trim, crop, text, music, stickers, keyframes, GIFs
- **Rewynd Online** — share clips with friends, likes, comments, @tags, views
- Mic & camera kill switch for every app

## Install

1. Download **[Rewynd-Setup.exe](https://github.com/aadomy21/Rewynd/releases/latest/download/Rewynd-Setup.exe)**
2. Open it. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway** (the app isn't signed with a paid certificate).
3. Click **Install**. Rewynd starts in the tray — press **Alt+Z** to open the overlay.

No admin rights or extra downloads needed. Rewynd updates itself: when a new version is out it asks you, one click and it restarts.

**Uninstall:** Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Rewynd → Uninstall. Your recordings are kept.

Needs Windows 10 (2004 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit.
