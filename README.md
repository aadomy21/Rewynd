# Rewynd

Record, clip, edit, stream and share your games — light on your PC.

## Capture

- **Instant replay** — save the last moments after something cool happens (Alt+C), from a controller (View + RB), or just say "clip that"
- **Auto clips** — Rewynd notices big moments and saves them by itself while you play, plus kills, aces and wins in League of Legends and CS2
- **Record** full screen, one app, or just an area you drag — each sound on its own track
- **Screenshots** of the whole screen or just an area, copied so you can paste them anywhere
- **Facecam** in a corner of your recordings, and mic noise removal for fans and hum
- **Game detection** — Rewynd starts instant replay when a game opens, and shows a recap when it closes
- **FPS counter** and performance stats on screen while you play
- **Up to 4K, or up to 144 fps**
- **Phone remote** — save clips, play sounds and switch voices from your phone on the same Wi-Fi

## Edit

- **Quick trim** right from the "Clip saved" pop-up — instant, no quality lost
- **Editor + Studio** — trim, crop, text, music, stickers, keyframes, GIFs
- **Make it vertical** for TikTok, Shorts and Reels, with a facecam box on top
- **Meme captions**, as a video or a GIF
- **Montages** cut to the beat of your song, and automatic **session recaps**
- **Auto captions** — what you say, on screen, made offline
- **Import clips** from Xbox Game Bar, ShadowPlay, AMD ReLive, Medal and Outplayed

## Share

- **Fit for Discord** — shrinks a clip under the upload limit and copies it, ready to paste
- **Post to a Discord channel** automatically, and show what you're playing on your Discord profile
- **The Lobby** — share clips with friends: feed, likes, reactions, comments with timestamps, @tags, Clip of the Week
- **Friends & chats** — message friends, send pictures, GIFs and clips
- **Post on TikTok, Instagram, X or Reddit** in a couple of clicks
- **Profiles** with a picture, banner, level, badges, collections and hours recorded
- **See what friends are playing**, weekly quests, and the most clipped games

## Live

- **Go live on YouTube** with your own stream key, or on **any RTMP server** like OBS does
- **Go live in the Lobby** — stream inside Rewynd for your friends, people you tag, or everyone
- **Overlays** — text, a live timer, your logo, YouTube chat, and Streamlabs / StreamElements alerts for donations, subs and goals
- **Edit layout** — drag and resize everything over a picture of your screen, with a live preview of what viewers see
- Your streams show at the top of your friends' feed, and recording and instant replay keep working while you're live

## Voice

- **Voice changer** — 24 voices, plus your own made in Voicelab
- **Soundboard** — starter sounds, your own files, sounds you record, or the last 10 seconds you heard
- **Text to speech** into your mic
- **Sounds & voices market** — share yours and add other people's
- Works in Discord and games through a virtual mic
- **Sound setup** — pick your mic (phone mics through MicStream and similar apps too) and what you hear from
- **Bluetooth headphones** — connect and disconnect them from Rewynd, and see their battery

## Make it yours

- **Themes** — presets, your own colors, a picture background, UI sounds
- **Theme market** — install themes other people made, or share yours
- **Library** — search, filter by game, star your favorites
- **Storage cleaner** — keeps the folder under a size you pick (starred clips are never touched)
- **Your layout** — resize widgets, snap them together, pick which is on top, stand the top bar up on the side
- **Battery on the top bar** — your laptop's and your Bluetooth headphones'
- Mic & camera kill switch for every app

## Hotkeys

| Action | Default |
|---|---|
| Open / close the overlay | Alt+Z |
| Save a clip | Alt+C |
| Instant replay on / off | Ctrl+Alt+B |
| Start / stop recording | Alt+R |
| Pause / resume recording | Ctrl+Alt+P |
| Screenshot | Ctrl+Alt+S |
| Screenshot an area | Ctrl+Alt+A |
| Mic on / off for all apps | Ctrl+Alt+M |
| Camera on / off for all apps | Ctrl+Alt+K |
| Voice changer on / off | Ctrl+Alt+V |
| Stop soundboard sounds | Ctrl+Alt+X |

Change them any time in Settings.

## Install

1. Download **[Rewynd-Setup.exe](https://github.com/aadomy21/Rewynd/releases/latest/download/Rewynd-Setup.exe)**
2. Open it. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway** (the app isn't signed with a paid certificate).
3. Click **Install**. Rewynd starts in the tray — press **Alt+Z** to open the overlay.

No admin rights or extra downloads needed. Rewynd updates itself: when a new version is out it asks you, one click and it restarts.

**Uninstall:** Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Rewynd → Uninstall. Your recordings are kept.

Needs Windows 10 (2004 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit.
