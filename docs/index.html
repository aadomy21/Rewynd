Instant replay

It's already recording. You decide what to keep.

While you play, Rewynd quietly keeps the last 5 minutes and lets older footage go, so nothing piles up on your drive. When something happens, you save it after the fact, then trim it to the moment. The demo above does the same with 8 seconds.

Everything in one overlay

Record, edit, share, stream, talk.

Press Alt+Z over any game and it's all there, in widgets you can move, resize and snap together.

CaptureFull screen, one app, or an area you drag.

  • Up to 4K, or up to 144 fpsEach sound on its own track: game, everything else, and your mic.
  • ScreenshotsOf the whole screen or an area, copied so you can paste them anywhere.
  • FacecamIn a corner of your recordings, with mic noise removal for fans and hum.
  • Game detectionInstant replay starts when a game opens, and you get a recap when it closes.

EditQuick fixes or a full studio.

  • Editor and StudioTrim, crop, text, music, stickers, keyframes and GIFs.
  • Make it verticalFor TikTok, Shorts and Reels, with your facecam in a box on top.
  • Auto captionsWhat you say, on screen, made offline on your PC.
  • MontagesCut to the beat of your song, plus automatic session recaps and meme captions.

ShareWith friends first.

  • The LobbyA feed for you and your friends: likes, reactions, comments with timestamps, @tags and Clip of the Week.
  • Fit for DiscordShrinks a clip under the upload limit and copies it, ready to paste.
  • Friends and chatsMessage friends, send pictures, GIFs and clips, and see what they're playing.
  • Post anywhereTikTok, Instagram, X or Reddit in a couple of clicks.

LiveStream without another app.

  • YouTube or any RTMP serverUse your own stream key, the way OBS does.
  • Live in the LobbyStream inside Rewynd for your friends, people you tag, or everyone.
  • OverlaysText, a live timer, your logo, YouTube chat, and alerts for donations, subs and goals.
  • Keeps recordingInstant replay and recording keep working while you're live.

VoiceWhat your friends hear.

  • Voice changer24 voices, plus your own made in Voicelab.

Make it yoursYour layout, your look.

Install

Ready in a minute.

  1. Download Rewynd-Setup.exe

    About 210 MB, straight from the official GitHub releases.

  2. Open it

    If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Rewynd isn't signed with a paid certificate.

  3. Click Install

    Rewynd starts in the tray. Press Alt+Z in any game to open the overlay.
